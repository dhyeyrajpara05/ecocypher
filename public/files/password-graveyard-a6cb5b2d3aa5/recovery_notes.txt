RECOVERY NOTES
==============

The dump was copied from an old system. The strings are not encrypted with
a secret key. They have been transformed using simple reversible operations.

Possible operations used in this dump:
- Base64 encoding
- hexadecimal representation of text bytes
- ROT13 letter substitution
- reversing the character order

A record may have gone through more than one operation. When operations are
layered, undo them in the reverse order in which they were applied.

The account directory and password dump use the same record_id.
Not every recovered password belongs to a currently active account.

For this exercise, identify the password associated with the active
administrator account. Do not assume that the most obvious decoded string
is the answer.
